# Engagement Guide

This guide covers common client engagement artifacts and expectations for pentesting projects.

## Scope definition
- Establish targets and exclusions.
- Define testing windows and environmental constraints.
- Confirm legal authorization.

## Deliverables
- Statement of Work
- Executive Summary
- Technical Report
- Retesting guidance

## Communication
- Keep stakeholders informed of critical findings.
- Use secure channels for sensitive information.
- Provide periodic status updates during active testing.
