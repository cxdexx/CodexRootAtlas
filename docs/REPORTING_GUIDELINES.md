# Reporting Guidelines

Reports should be concise, actionable, and structured for both technical and business audiences.

## Finding format
- Title
- Severity
- Affected asset(s)
- Description
- Impact
- Reproduction steps
- Evidence
- Mitigation

## Severity categories
- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low
- Info

## Executive summary
- High-level risk overview
- Impact on business objectives
- Recommended actions

## Appendices
- Tools used
- Scopes and exclusions
- Environment details
