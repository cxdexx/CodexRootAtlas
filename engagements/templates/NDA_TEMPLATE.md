# Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) Template

This NDA template is provided as a starting point for client engagements.

## Parties
- Discloser: [Client Name]
- Recipient: [Tester / Vendor Name]

## Purpose
- Protect confidential information exchanged during the engagement.

## Term
- Effective from [Start Date]
- Duration: [Number of years]

## Confidential information
- All findings, communications, systems, and documentation.

## Obligations
- Maintain confidentiality
- Use information only for authorized purposes
- Return or destroy sensitive materials after engagement
