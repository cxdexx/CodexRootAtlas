1. The Magic Handshake ($RTT$)

Before you get your toys, your computer and the store server have to say "Hello" to each other. Under old systems, they had to go back and forth 3 times just to agree it was safe to talk! With modern HTTP/3, they do the connection handshake and secret locking setup in just **one single trip**!

2. The Train Traffic Jam (HoL Blocking)

Imagine your web files are train cars linked together. If one train car hits a bump and falls off the track (Packet Loss), **every single toy** on that train has to stop and wait on the track until the broken car is rescued. With QUIC, we use independent flying drones instead, so one drone crashing doesn't stop the others!

3. Secret Portal Doors (Ports)

Web servers are like huge castle walls with thousands of secret delivery doors. Standard internet web pages always go through Door **80** (open) or Door **443** (securely locked). When system builders want to hide or work on things privately, they use back doors like **8080** or **8443**.
